# Sundown Pacific Storefront Display Rules

## Default shirt presentation

- Use a simple catalog-style product image.
- Show one large back-view shirt as the main image.
- Show one smaller front-view shirt overlapping at the lower left.
- Keep the shirt color, fabric, lighting, and background consistent within the card.
- Use the approved production artwork without redrawing it.
- Do not use models, lifestyle scenes, extra slogans, or alternate layouts unless the owner specifically requests them for that design.
- Preview and approve each new card before publishing it.
